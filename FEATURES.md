# HardwarePro Lite v1.0.0 – Feature Overview

HardwarePro is an offline-first Windows desktop POS and management system for hardware shops and building-material businesses.

## Dashboard
- Sales and business overview
- Gross profit indicators
- Credit due
- Purchases
- Low-stock visibility
- Pending cheques
- Quotation activity

## POS / Sales
- Keyboard-friendly checkout
- Barcode / product code / name search
- Retail, Wholesale and Contractor pricing
- Minimum selling price control
- Manager approval for protected pricing
- Cash, Card, Bank Transfer, Cheque and LANKAQR recording
- Partial and credit payments

## Products & Stock
- Products, categories and brands
- Base-unit stock storage
- Multiple selling units
- Conversion factors
- Stock adjustments
- Damaged stock
- Stock reservations for Sales Orders
- Excel product import

## Customers & Credit
- Customer accounts
- Credit limits
- Opening balances
- Payment collection
- Running ledger
- Invoice / return balance tracking
- Authorized credit-limit overrides

## Quotations & Sales Orders
- Quotations
- Discounts and delivery charges
- Convert quotation to Sales Order
- Convert quotation to Invoice
- Stock reservation by confirmed order

## Delivery Notes
- Dispatch workflow
- Vehicle details
- Driver details
- Link delivery activity to the sales workflow

## Purchases / GRN & Suppliers
- Supplier accounts
- GRN / purchase receiving
- Supplier credit
- Supplier payments
- Purchase returns

## Cheques
- Cheque recording and management
- Pending cheque visibility

## Expenses & Day-End
- Business expense recording
- Opening cash
- Day closing
- Expected vs actual cash handling
- Audit visibility for authorized corrections

## Reports
- Sales
- Gross profit
- Stock valuation
- Customer outstanding
- Supplier outstanding
- Purchases
- Cheques
- Expenses

## Export & Printing
- PDF export
- Excel (.xlsx) export
- A4 invoice
- A5 invoice
- 80mm thermal receipt / invoice
- Quotations
- Delivery notes
- Customer statements

## Users & Security Controls
- Owner
- Manager
- Cashier
- Storekeeper
- Manager PIN approval
- Audit log

## Data & Backup
- Local SQLite database
- Backup
- Restore
- Offline-first daily operation

## Hardware-oriented units
Supported units include Piece, Kg, Feet, Meter, Litre, Bag, Box, Sheet, Coil and Length.
