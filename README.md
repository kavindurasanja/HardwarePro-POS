## HardwarePro at a glance

**HardwarePro Lite v1.0.0** is a desktop POS and business management system designed for hardware stores, building-material shops, construction-supply businesses and similar retail/wholesale operations.

It is built for a **single Windows PC** and stores business data locally in a **SQLite database**, so normal billing, stock control, credit management, purchases, reports and backups can continue without a permanent internet connection.

### Core workflow

`Quotation → Sales Order → Delivery Note → Invoice → Payment / Credit → Customer Ledger`

## Key features

- Fast keyboard-friendly POS / checkout
- Product, category and brand management
- Hardware-specific units: Piece, Kg, Feet, Meter, Litre, Bag, Box, Sheet, Coil and Length
- Multiple selling units with conversion factors
- Retail, Wholesale, Contractor and Minimum Selling Price levels
- Manager PIN approval for protected pricing and credit overrides
- Real-time stock control, stock adjustments and damaged stock
- Stock reservation for open / confirmed Sales Orders
- Customer accounts, credit limits, payment collection and ledger
- Quotations with conversion to Sales Order or Invoice
- Delivery Notes with vehicle and driver details
- Supplier accounts, purchases / GRN, supplier credit and payments
- Cheque management
- Sales returns and purchase returns
- Expenses and day-end cash handling
- Sales, gross profit, stock valuation and outstanding reports
- PDF and Excel export
- A4, A5 and 80mm invoice / receipt output
- Owner, Manager, Cashier and Storekeeper user roles
- Audit log
- Local backup and restore
- Configurable English / Sinhala receipt footer

## Designed for hardware & building-material businesses

HardwarePro handles workflows that general-purpose POS systems often make awkward. Products can be stocked in a base unit and sold using practical hardware-store units and conversion factors.

Examples:

| Product | Base unit | Selling unit example |
|---|---|---|
| GI Pipe | Feet | Length |
| Nails | Kg | Bag |
| Cement | Bag | Bag |
| Wire | Meter | Coil |
| Fasteners | Piece | Box |

A product stored as **Piece** can, for example, be sold as **Box** using a configured conversion factor while stock is deducted correctly from the base quantity.

## Customer credit & ledger

HardwarePro supports customer credit limits, partial payments, opening balances, invoice balances, returns and running ledger balances.

When a credit sale exceeds the configured limit, authorized Owner / Manager approval can be required before continuing.

## Quotations, orders & delivery

Create quotations with products, quantities, prices, discounts and delivery charges, then continue the workflow without re-entering the same data.

**Quotation → Sales Order → Delivery Note → Invoice**

Sales Orders can reserve stock before invoicing, while Delivery Notes can record dispatch information such as vehicle and driver details.

## Purchases, suppliers & GRN

Receive supplier stock through the Purchase / GRN workflow, track supplier credit, record supplier payments and process purchase returns through the proper return workflow.

## Reports & exports

Available business reporting includes:

- Sales
- Gross profit
- Stock valuation
- Customer outstanding
- Supplier outstanding
- Purchases
- Cheques
- Expenses

Supported outputs include **PDF** and **Excel (.xlsx)** where applicable.

## Printing

HardwarePro supports business documents and invoice / receipt output for:

- A4
- A5
- 80mm thermal printers

Printer layout should always be tested with the exact printer model before live deployment.

## Offline-first Windows software

HardwarePro Lite is intended for **Windows 10 / 11 x64** and normal business operations do not require cloud hosting, Firebase or an AI API.

Business data is stored on the client PC, so regular backups are strongly recommended.
